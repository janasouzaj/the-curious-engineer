# The Curious Engineer Roadmap

I'm not building a resume. I'm documenting my evolution as an engineer! Welcome to **The Curious Engineer**, this repository documents my 24-month journey to deepen my knowledge in 
Platform Engineering, Observability, Cloud Native technologies, Distributed Systems, and Technical Leadership. The goal isn't to complete courses or collect certificates, the goal is to build a
strong engineering foundation through books, official documentation, hands-on labs, technical writing, and real-world projects.

---

# Philosophy

This roadmap follows a simple principle: **Learn → Build → Document → Share**
