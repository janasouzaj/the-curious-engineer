## October 7, 2026 — Restart

I paused this project for a while and came back to it with a clearer career direction.

I still want to work with DevOps, but now the specialization is explicit: **Observability**.

The baseline assessment also showed something important: I already have practical exposure to several tools, but I need stronger fundamentals underneath them.

The new goal is not to restart from zero.

It is to understand more deeply what I already touch in production.

That means learning to connect:

```text
Linux
  ↓
Networking
  ↓
Containers
  ↓
Kubernetes
  ↓
Cloud
  ↓
Telemetry
  ↓
Reliability
  ↓
Architecture
```
